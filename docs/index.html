01. Overview & Scope of Application

Vargani Collection ("we", "us", "our") is a digital record-keeping and ledger utility designed for cultural committees, Ganesh mandals, Navratri samitis, and community groups. It is operated and published by Ritesh Sangale.

By downloading, installing, or registering an account on Vargani Collection, users acknowledge having reviewed, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy. If an organizer or individual does not agree, they must cease usage of the application.

Statutory Non-Financial & Non-Banking Clarification
Vargani Collection functions solely as an administrative record-keeping ledger. It is NOT a financial institution, non-banking financial company (NBFC), digital wallet, or payment aggregator. The platform does not directly initiate, clear, settle, or hold funds. All transactions occur peer-to-peer (via cash or external UPI tools such as BHIM, Google Pay, or PhonePe). Vargani Collection generates digital receipts and computes balance sheets.

02. Categories of Data Collected

In accordance with the principle of data minimization, the application collects only information strictly necessary to provide bookkeeping, member directory, and receipt generation functionalities:

Category Data Elements Collected Operational Purpose Retention Period
Account & Profile Full name, phone number, email address, mandal/organization name. User authentication, account management, multi-user role access. Until account deletion requested by user.
Contributor Information Family head name, contact number, residential locality/wing, address. Generating individualized contribution receipts and mandal directory. Retained per mandal record cycle or until deleted.
Contribution Records Amount contributed, date, payment method (Cash / UPI), optional reference ID. Auditing, transparent balance-sheet calculations, duplicate avoidance. Maintained for financial transparency of the respective mandal.
Operational Expenses Expense category, payment recipient, voucher photos, amount expended. Preparation of downloadable balance sheets and financial accountability. Maintained for financial transparency of the respective mandal.
Device & Diagnostics Operating system version, device model, anonymized crash traces. Ensuring app stability, bug resolution, and security defense. Up to 90 days in aggregated format.

03. Purpose and Legal Basis of Processing

We process collected data exclusively under the following legal bases:

  • Contractual & Service Fulfillment: To authenticate organizers, record incoming contributions, generate branded digital PDF receipts, and calculate committee balance sheets.
  • Statutory & Legal Accountability: To prevent fraud, detect duplicate financial entries, and maintain auditable bookkeeping records for cultural trusts.
  • Legitimate Interests: To troubleshoot crashes, optimize user interface performance, and protect server infrastructure against denial-of-service abuse.

Commercial Guarantee: We enforce an unyielding policy prohibiting the sale, rental, barter, or commercialization of member names, phone numbers, or contribution records to third-party marketing brokers, loan aggregators, or unauthorized external entities.

04. Third-Party Service Providers and Infrastructure

To deliver reliable cloud services, we partner with industry-standard, security-certified cloud vendors:

  • Supabase Inc. (Backend Cloud & Authentication): Data is stored in secure cloud-hosted PostgreSQL instances with Row Level Security (RLS) enforcement. All transmissions are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3 and at rest via AES-256.
  • Google Play Services & Google AdMob (Google LLC): Facilitates app distribution, essential runtime libraries, and displays non-intrusive mobile banner advertisements. AdMob operates under Google's Privacy & Terms.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue): Utilized solely for transactional delivery of authentication verification emails and password reset links.

05. Android System Permissions

Vargani Collection requests only operational system permissions on Android devices:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to synchronize ledger records securely with cloud databases.
  • STORAGE / MEDIA ACCESS: Required only when the user chooses to download PDF receipts or export Excel balance sheets to local device storage.
  • CAMERA / PHOTO LIBRARY: Accessed solely if an administrator elects to photograph a paper expense voucher or upload a custom mandal UPI QR image.
