1. Legal Nature & Financial Disclaimer

IMPORTANT REGULATORY CLARIFICATION:
Vargani Collection is strictly an organization and bookkeeping record-management utility.
• We are NOT a bank, Non-Banking Financial Company (NBFC), digital wallet, money transmitter, or payment gateway.
• We do NOT process financial loans, peer-to-peer credit, or hold public money.
• The application only provides an electronic ledger, automated PDF receipts, and balance-sheet calculation tools for festival mandals (e.g., Ganeshotsav, Navratri, Shiv Jayanti) and community welfare groups.
• Any payments made between contributors and mandal organizers take place entirely offline (cash) or through third-party UPI applications (e.g., Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm). We do not store bank passwords, ATM PINs, or CVVs.

2. Information We Collect

To provide digital receipt generation, mandal ledger maintenance, and secure authentication, we collect the following categories of data:

Category Data Elements Collected Primary Purpose
Personal Information Full Name, Mobile Number, Email Address, Optional Profile Photo Account creation, worker authentication, and personalized receipt issuance.
Mandal & Family Data Mandal Name, Address, Contributor/Family Head Name, House Number, Member Count Organizing contributor directories and calculating annual contributions.
Transaction Records Amount Paid, Payment Mode (Cash/UPI), Date/Time, UPI UTR / Transaction Reference Number Generating official PDF receipts, balance sheets, and preventing duplicate payment claims.
Expense & Voucher Data Expense Category (Mandap, Sound, Food, etc.), Payee Name, Amount, Expense Notes Transparent audit reports, treasury tracking, and balance sheet exports.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

Our Android application requests only the minimal system permissions required to deliver essential features:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to securely connect to our encrypted backend database (Supabase) via HTTPS to save and synchronize your records in real time.
  • READ & WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE (Android 12 and below) / Storage Access: Required strictly to download and save generated PDF receipts and Excel balance sheets to your device storage.
  • CAMERA / PHOTO GALLERY: Used solely if the user voluntarily chooses to upload a Mandal UPI QR code image or attach an expense bill voucher. We do not access background photos or private media.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry-leading service providers who maintain rigorous security and privacy standards:

  • Supabase Inc. (Backend Database & Authentication): All user profiles, mandal ledger entries, and receipts are stored in Supabase managed databases using TLS 1.3 encryption in transit and AES-256 encryption at rest. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob: We display banner ads to support development and free hosting. Google AdMob may collect anonymized advertising identifiers (GAID) and crash analytics. Google Privacy Policy and AdMob Policies.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue - Transactional Email): Used exclusively to deliver verification emails and password reset codes. Brevo Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security Standards

Zero Commercial Sale of Data:
We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal data, contributor phone numbers, or mandal financial information to telemarketers, credit bureaus, or advertising aggregators. Your mandal records belong exclusively to your mandal.

We implement industry-grade technical and organizational safeguards:

  • End-to-end encrypted transport layer (HTTPS/TLS) for all communications.
  • Strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring members of one mandal cannot view or alter records of another mandal.
  • Bcrypt hashing for user passwords (passwords are never saved in plain text).
