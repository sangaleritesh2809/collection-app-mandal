Types of Data Deleted vs. Retained

Google Play requires transparent declaration of what data is purged and what data is legally retained:

Data Category Exact Information Status After Request Timeline / Reason
Personal Credentials Full name, phone number, email ID, encrypted password hash, auth tokens. PERMANENTLY DELETED Purged from production database within 7 business days.
Admin & Member Roles Committee admin associations, permissions, member invitations. PERMANENTLY DELETED Revoked immediately upon processing.
Device Information Push notification tokens, device identifiers, session cookies. PERMANENTLY DELETED Wiped immediately from backend.
Mandal Collective Tallies Aggregated monetary totals on published mandal balance sheets (without personal donor names). ANONYMIZED TALLY Mathematical balance-sheet totals are preserved strictly for community audit transparency, stripped of any individual personal identifiers.

Processing Timeline & Retention Policy

  • Verification: We verify the request within 24 to 48 business hours.
  • Production Purge: All personal credentials, individual profiles, and login permissions are completely erased within 7 business days.
  • Backup Retention: Encrypted system database snapshots overwrite automatically within a rolling 30-day disaster recovery retention cycle, after which no copies remain.
